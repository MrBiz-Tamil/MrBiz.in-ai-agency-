# Instagram, Facebook and Website Flows

Goal: turn attention into a WhatsApp conversation, quickly, without spamming anyone.

> Platform rules change. Before switching on automation, read the current Instagram and Meta
> messaging policies (especially the **24-hour messaging window**, comment-to-DM limits, and
> automated-reply disclosure). If a rule here conflicts with Meta's current rule, Meta wins.

## Golden rules
1. **One automatic DM per comment, ever.** No repeated nudges.
2. **Always offer a human.** Every flow has a "Talk to a person" option.
3. **Be honest that it's automated** at the start ("I'm {{brand}}'s assistant").
4. **Move the sale to WhatsApp.** Instagram DMs are for the first hello; WhatsApp is where you close,
   confirm orders, and send updates.
5. **Tag every conversation with its source** (table at the end) so you can see what makes money.

---

## Flow 1: Comment-to-DM (your reels and posts)

**Setup:** in the caption or on the video, say: *"Comment **LINK** and I'll DM you the details."*

**Keywords to listen for** (case-insensitive): `LINK`, `PRICE`, `SIZE`, `DETAILS`, `BUY`

**Public reply (rotate these so it doesn't look robotic):**
1. "Sent you a DM! 💌"
2. "Check your DMs, I've sent the details ✨"
3. "Done! Look out for our message 🛍️"
4. "Just messaged you the link 💛"
5. "On its way to your inbox 📩"

**DM 1 (sent immediately):**
> Hi {{name}}! 👋 I'm {{brand}}'s assistant. Here's the item you asked about: {{product_link}}
> What would you like next?
> **[Check my size]  [See more like this]  [Talk to a person]**

**If "Check my size":**
> Happy to help! What's your usual size, or your height and weight? Our size chart: {{size_chart_link}}
> (then use FAQ #1–#2; AI suggests a size)

**If "See more like this":**
> Here are a few similar styles: {{collection_link}}. Want me to check any size for you?

**If "Talk to a person":**
> Sure! I'll bring in a teammate. We're available {{support_hours}}. 🙏  (hand over; see `04`)

**Move to WhatsApp (after they show interest):**
> Want faster help and order updates? Chat with us on WhatsApp: {{whatsapp_link}}

*WhatsApp link format:* `https://wa.me/<your-number-with-country-code>?text=Hi%20I%20saw%20your%20post`
(put the number without `+`, spaces, or dashes; the text after `text=` pre-fills their message).

## Flow 2: Story reply

**Trigger:** someone replies to a story, sends 🔥 or "price", or asks "available?".

> Hi {{name}}! Thanks for the love. 😊 Which item is it? Send me a screenshot or the name and I'll
> check your size and stock right away.

If they send a screenshot, a person or the AI looks up the item. If stock is confirmed:
> Yes, it's available in {{sizes}}. Here's the link: {{product_link}}. Want to order on WhatsApp so
> I can help you through it? {{whatsapp_link}}

---

## Flow 3: Size help (works on any channel)

Ask in this order, one question at a time:
1. "What size do you usually wear?" (+ brand, if they say)
2. "Do you like a fitted or relaxed fit?"
3. "Height and weight (optional)?"

AI answer pattern: *"I'd go with **M** for a relaxed fit. If you prefer it snug, **S**. Our size chart:
{{size_chart_link}}. If it doesn't fit, {{exchange_policy}}."*

If the AI is unsure, it says so and hands over; it never guesses.

## Flow 4: Click-to-WhatsApp ads (Facebook and Instagram)

Ads whose button opens a WhatsApp chat. Messages are free for 72 hours after the customer messages you
from such an ad (verified on Meta's pricing page), so reply fast and keep the whole conversation
inside that window.

**Ice-breaker buttons (shown when the chat opens):**
- "Check availability"
- "Help me pick a size"
- "Delivery and returns"
- "Talk to a person"

**Welcome message:**
> Hi! 👋 Thanks for messaging {{brand}}. I'm the assistant and I can help with sizes, stock and
> delivery. What are you looking for today?

**Qualifying questions (max three):**
1. "Which item caught your eye?" (they'll usually say or send a screenshot)
2. "What size do you need?"
3. "Which city / pincode should we deliver to?" (checks delivery and COD)

**Close:**
> Great, {{item}} in {{size}} is available. 🎉 Here's the link to order: {{product_link}}
> {{cod_available}} If you'd like help, I'm right here.

## Flow 5: Website

- **Floating "Chat on WhatsApp" button** on every product page. Pre-fill the message with the page:
  `Hi, I'm interested in {{product_name}}` (so you know which item without asking).
- **Cart page:** a small line: "Questions about sizes or delivery? Chat with us."
- **Checkout:** the two consent tick boxes from `02-whatsapp-templates.md`.
- **Thank-you page:** "We'll send updates on WhatsApp" + the order number.
- **Footer/Contact:** your WhatsApp link, Instagram, and support hours.

---

## Flow 6: Facebook Page

- **Auto-reply + ice breakers** same as Flow 4.
- **Comments:** reply to every comment within a day. Questions get a short answer plus "DM us for details".
- **Hide** spam and abuse; **never delete** honest complaints (reply to them calmly instead).
- **Away message** (outside hours):
  > Thanks for reaching out! We're away right now. We'll reply {{support_hours}}. If it's about an
  > order, please send your order number so we can help faster.

## Source tags (apply automatically in your inbox)

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `src-ig-comment` | Came from a reel/post comment flow |
| `src-ig-story` | Replied to a story |
| `src-ig-dm` | Messaged Instagram directly |
| `src-fb-ad` | Click-to-WhatsApp or Messenger ad |
| `src-fb-page` | Messaged the Facebook Page |
| `src-web-chat` | Used the website chat/button |
| `src-referral` | Came through a friend or review |

At the end of each week, count how many tagged chats became orders. Spend more on the source with the
best chat-to-order rate and less on the rest.
